PUPPET THEATRE · 14+

Theatre puppetry

You bring a sketch to the workshop. Then one day the character looks back at you.

For adults and teenagers aged 14+. We go from the first idea to a real theatre puppet and a future performance.

What we'll make

From the first idea to something real.

01

Sketch

We develop the character's personality, expression and proportions.

02

Head

Carve a base from XPS foam and shape the form.

03

Papier-mâché and painting

Gradually the character acquires a surface, expression and face.

04

Costume and movement

Complete the look, bring the puppet to life and work towards a performance.

This is an ongoing group rather than a one-off workshop. Continue from block to block towards a performance.
