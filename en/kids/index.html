Creative after-school classes in Tbilisi

Art sessions for children aged 5–12 before or after school. Children make moving cardboard toys, needle-felt with wool and craft with felt.

For afternoon school shifts

Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 10:00–11:30 or 11:30–13:00.

For morning school shifts

Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 16:00–17:30 or 17:30–19:00.

Prices

25 GEL for one session or 180 GEL for 12 sessions. Children can attend one session or both in a row. Sessions take place in the ArtHub workshop.

Teachers

Elena Rybina and Maria Kondratyeva.
